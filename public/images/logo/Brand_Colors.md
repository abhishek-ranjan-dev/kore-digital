# Kore Digital — Brand Spec Sheet
### Color Codes Note

For consistent use across web, print, and partner collateral.

---

## Primary — Navy Blue
- **Hex:** `#1B365D`
- **RGB:** 27, 54, 93
- **CMYK (print):** 100, 83, 30, 36

## Accent — Red
- **Hex:** `#C8102E`
- **RGB:** 200, 16, 46
- **CMYK (print):** 0, 100, 81, 4

---

**Usage:** Navy Blue is the primary color — globe and "Kore Digital" wordmark.
Accent Red is used only for the orbit arc. On dark backgrounds, use the
`*_Dark_BG` logo variants (a lightened blue for legibility); keep the `*_Light_BG`
navy versions for white/light surfaces.
